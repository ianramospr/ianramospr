Terms of use

Chao is a free iPhone app for reviewing your photos, one at a time.

Using Chao

You can swipe right to keep a photo or left to add it to a deletion queue. You can review that queue before asking iOS to delete photos. Chao requires access to your photo library to work.

Your photos and decisions

Your photos remain yours. You are responsible for choosing which photos to delete. Review your selection carefully before confirming a deletion in iOS. Deleted photos may be recoverable for a limited time through the Photos app, subject to Apple's settings and policies.
