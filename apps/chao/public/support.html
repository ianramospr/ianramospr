Help me understand the issue

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Chao version, and what happened. If you attach a screenshot, remove personal information and photos you don’t want to share.

Does swiping left delete a photo?

No. It adds the photo to a queue for review. Deletion is requested from that queue, and iOS asks you to confirm.

How do I change photo access?

Use your iPhone’s Settings app to review Chao’s Photos permission. If you selected limited access, you can also manage the selection from Chao’s photo-access controls.

Can I send a feature request?

Yes. Send your idea to the same email address.
