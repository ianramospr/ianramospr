Does swiping left delete a photo?

No. It puts the photo in your deletion queue. Review the queue and confirm deletion through iOS when you're ready.

How do I change photo access?

Open iPhone Settings, find Chao, and change its Photos permission. If you grant access to selected photos only, Chao can show only those selected items.

Can I undo a swipe?

Use Undo on the review screen to reverse your most recent swipe.
