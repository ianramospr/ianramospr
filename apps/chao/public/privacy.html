Chao / Privacy

Privacy policy

Your photos stay yours. Chao helps you review your iPhone photo library without creating an account.

Photos and permissions

Chao asks for access to your Photos library so you can view photos, decide which to keep, and select others for deletion. You control photo access through iOS Settings.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photos and their available details, such as dates and locations, are used to show and organize them in the app. Your review progress and deletion queue are stored on your device. Chao does not upload your photo library to a server.

Deleting photos

Swiping left adds a photo to a review queue. It does not delete the photo immediately. When you choose to delete queued photos, iOS asks you to confirm. You can manage deleted photos using Apple's Photos app, including Recently Deleted where available.

Data collection

Chao does not require sign-in and is not designed to collect personal information, track you, or sell your data. It does not use advertising. This describes the app's intended on-device operation.

Your choices

You can change or revoke Chao's Photos permission at any time in iOS Settings. You can also remove the app and its local app data by uninstalling it; photos in your Photos library are separate from Chao's app data.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, email hello@ianramospr.com.
